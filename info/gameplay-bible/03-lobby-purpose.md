# Saints Gaming — Gameplay Bible: Lobby Purpose (Draft v0.1)

# The Lobby Identity

## The Lobby is the Heart of Saints Gaming

The lobby is not a menu.

It is not a loading screen.

It is not a temporary waiting room.

**The lobby is the player's home within the world.**

Every player begins here, returns here, socializes here, prepares here, and grows here.

The lobby is the first proof that Saints Gaming is a living MMO.

---

# The Lobby Purpose

The lobby exists to connect every major system in the game.

It is the bridge between:

* Exploration
* Combat
* Creatures
* Skills
* Building
* Social systems
* Player creation

The player should always know:

**"What can I do next?"**

The lobby answers that.

---

# The Player Experience

A new player enters the lobby.

They see:

* Other players moving around
* Creatures being shown off
* Player shops
* Community creations
* Activity happening around them

They learn by existing in the world, not by sitting in menus.

---

# Core Lobby Functions

## 1. Social Hub

The lobby is where players meet.

Players can:

* Chat
* Create parties
* Trade
* Show creatures
* Visit friends
* Join activities

Social interaction should happen naturally.

---

# 2. Player Progression Center

The lobby is where players manage their journey.

Access:

* Skills
* Inventory
* Equipment
* Creature collection
* Quests
* Achievements
* Statistics

The player can see their entire progression from one location.

---

# 3. Adventure Gateway

The lobby connects players to the world.

Locations:

* Wild areas
* Towns
* Dungeons
* Events
* Player bases
* Community maps

Travel should feel like leaving a real place, not clicking a button.

---

# 4. Party Formation Area

Multiplayer begins here.

Players can:

* Create groups
* Recruit teammates
* View party members
* Prepare builds
* Choose creatures

The lobby encourages cooperation before adventure starts.

---

# 5. Creature Showcase

Creatures are a major identity feature.

The lobby allows players to display:

* Rare creatures
* Fusions
* Evolutions
* Achievements

Other players should immediately see:

"That player has something special."

---

# 6. Player Economy

The lobby supports social trading.

Possible systems:

* Player shops
* Trading areas
* Auction systems
* Crafting displays
* Resource exchange

The lobby becomes the marketplace of the world.

---

# 7. Creator Showcase

This is the unique Saints Gaming feature.

The lobby displays what players create.

Examples:

* Custom maps
* Builds
* Events
* Player-designed spaces

Creators gain visibility.

Players discover new content.

---

# Technical Purpose

The lobby is also the first complete test of the MMO framework.

It validates:

* Multiplayer synchronization
* Player spawning
* Chat
* Parties
* UI persistence
* Object placement
* NPC systems
* World transitions
* Saved player data

If the lobby works, the foundation works.

---

# UI Philosophy

The lobby UI should feel like part of the world.

Avoid:

* Separate disconnected menus
* Excessive popups
* Hidden systems

Prefer:

* Persistent HUD
* Expandable panels
* Player-controlled layouts
* Saved UI customization

Players should be able to arrange their interface.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The lobby evolves over time.

Early:

* Spawn area
* Basic services
* Social interaction

Later:

* Player markets
* Guild spaces
* Events
* Creator showcases
* Community-built districts

Eventually:

**The lobby becomes a living city created by the community.**

---

# Final Lobby Rule

**The player should never feel like they are waiting to play.**

The moment they enter Saints Gaming, they are already inside the game.
