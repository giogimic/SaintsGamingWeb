## Quest 1: Tools of the Trade

**Objective:** Learn movement, tile/node interaction, and acquire fundamental survival gear.

> **Quest Log:** You wake up on the outskirts of **Emberwood Basin**. The wilderness ahead is full of untamed resources and wild elemental creatures. You'll need more than bare hands if you plan to survive out here.

### Walkthrough Steps

1. **Talk to Warden Vance**
   - Click **Warden Vance** near the starting camp spawn tile.
   - *Dialogue:* "Out here, nature yields only to those with the right edge. Take this kit. You'll need to chop, dig, and fish if you want to craft anything worth carrying."

2. **Receive starter items** — unlock the **Starter Toolbelt**:
   - **Rook Hatchet** (Woodcutting Level 1)
   - **Crude Pickaxe** (Mining Level 1)
   - **Mesh Net** (Fishing Level 1)
   - **Flint Tinderbox** (Firemaking Level 1)

3. **Gather Raw Materials**
   - **Node 1:** Pine Tree → chop 3 **Pine Logs**
   - **Node 2:** Copper Vein → mine 3 **Copper Ores**
   - **Node 3:** Rippling Shallows → net 1 **Freshwater Minnow**

---

## Quest 2: Forging the Vessel

**Objective:** Experience the crafting loop and produce first creature-capture items.

> **Quest Log:** Gathering raw materials is only half the battle. To tame the untamed beasts of this realm, you must forge containers capable of channeling wild elemental essence.

### Walkthrough Steps

1. **Process materials**
   - Use **Flint Tinderbox** + **Pine Log** → campfire
   - Cook **Freshwater Minnow** on fire → **Seared Minnow** (Cooking XP)

2. **Head to the Smithy**
   - Walk to **Basin Anvil**
   - Smelt 3 **Copper Ores** → 1 **Copper Ingot**
   - Craft ingot on anvil → 3 **Empty Core Capsules**

3. **Report to Warden Vance**
   - *Dialogue:* "Impressive work. A solid vessel, but an empty shell means nothing. Now it's time to choose the companion that will walk this path with you."

---

## Quest 3: The First Bond

**Objective:** Choose starter companion, learn TB battle, complete first capture.

> **Quest Log:** Deep inside the Grove Sanctuary, three distinct elemental aura signatures have been detected. Step inside and forge a lifelong bond with your starter familiar.

### Walkthrough Steps

1. Enter **Grove Sanctuary** (instanced trial)
2. Choose starter nest:
   - 🔥 **Ignis Scrub:** Pyrodrake Hatchling (Fire / Strength)
   - 🌿 **Verdant Sprout:** Thornhide Calf (Nature / Endurance)
   - 💧 **River Pebble:** Current Otter (Water / Agility)
3. Nest selection triggers simulated battle
4. Weaken → throw **Empty Core Capsule** → capture
5. Feed companion **Seared Minnow** (vitality + Loyalty)

## Quest 4: Wilderness Clearance

**Objective:** Combine gathering + companion abilities to unlock the full demo map.

> **Quest Log:** A thick wall of overgrown Bramble Vines blocks the bridge to the main world map. Standard tools aren't enough—you'll need to work alongside your new companion to clear the path.

```
[OBSTACLE]: Entangled Bramble Wall (Level 1 Synergy Event)
```

### Walkthrough Steps

1. Interact with **Overgrown Bramble Wall**
2. Dual interaction: **Rook Hatchet** + Familiar elemental ability
3. Rewards: 100 Coins, 5× Standard Core Capsules, **Aethervale Map Access**
